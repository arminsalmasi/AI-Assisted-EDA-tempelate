DATA QUALITY AUDIT REPORT & CLEANING PLAN
================================================================================

This document details the findings of a comprehensive quality audit conducted on 
the processed datasets (`master_orders.csv` and `customer_features.csv`) and 
outlines the handling plan for the upcoming exploratory data analysis (EDA).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. QUALITY AUDIT FINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- **Missing Values**: 
  *Result*: **0 missing values**. 
  All columns across both the order-level and customer-level datasets are 100% 
  complete.

- **Duplicate Rows**: 
  *Result*: **0 duplicate rows**. 
  Every record in `master_orders.csv` and `customer_features.csv` represents a 
  unique entity/transaction.

- **Chronological Consistency**:
  *Result*: **100% Consistent**.
  - All orders are placed strictly *on or after* the customer's signup date 
    (no negative signup-to-order deltas).
  - All customers have a non-negative active lifespan (`active_lifespan_days >= 0`).

- **Numeric Bounds & Financial Integrity**:
  *Result*: **100% Consistent**.
  - No negative order values (`order_value_usd`), discount amounts 
    (`discount_amount_usd`), or item counts (`items_count`).
  - No discounts exceed the gross order value (`discount_amount_usd <= order_value_usd`).
  - Mismatched delay minutes calculations check: **0 mismatches**. Calculated 
    delay (`max(0, actual_time_min - promised_time_min)`) perfectly matches the 
    `delay_minutes` field.
  - All customer ratings are valid integers within the standard `[1, 5]` range.

- **Categorical Integrity & Standardization**:
  *Result*: **Clean and Standardized**.
  - All categorical features (`vendor_category`, `delivery_type`, `preferred_device`, 
    `customer_segment`, `gender`, `age_group`, `city`, `state`) have uniform casing, 
    no trailing whitespaces, and no spelling variations.

- **Outlier Audit Summary**:
  - `Max items count`: 24 items (valid for grocery basket sizes).
  - `Max order value`: $342.21 (valid for larger retail/grocery shopping).
  - `Max delivery delay`: 130 minutes (severe but realistic delay in delivery).
  - `Max orders frequency`: 126 orders over the 2.3-year tracking span (active users).
  *Result*: High values are realistic business exceptions rather than corrupted entries.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DATA CLEANING & EDA HANDLING PLAN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Since the dataset has zero missing values, duplicate entries, or corruption anomalies, 
no corrective deletion or imputation is required. Instead, our cleaning and handling 
strategy will focus on preparation for modeling and analysis:

1. **Date Time Parsing**:
   *Issue*: CSV format stores dates (`order_date`, `signup_date`, `latest_order_date`) 
   as string text.
   *Action*: Convert these columns to Pandas datetime format immediately upon loading 
   them in notebooks or analysis scripts.

2. **Extreme Outlier Management**:
   *Issue*: Highly active/high-spending customers and severe delays (e.g. 130 minutes) 
   create a heavily skewed distribution.
   *Action*:
   - Do **not** delete these records, as they represent real customer experiences and 
     premium revenue segments.
   - For statistical modeling (like regressions), apply log transforms to monetary values.
   - Use median and percentile ranges (IQRs) for operational benchmarks rather than simple 
     averages, to avoid average inflation by extreme delays.

3. **Behavioral Churn Verification**:
   *Action*: Confirm that the behavioral churn definition (90 days of inactivity from 
   the latest date in the dataset) does not misclassify late signups (customers who 
   signed up in the last 90 days). In our preprocessing script `prepare_data.py`, new 
   customers with no orders for 90 days are marked as churned, which is correct, but 
   we will visualize cohorts to separate sign-up tenure.
