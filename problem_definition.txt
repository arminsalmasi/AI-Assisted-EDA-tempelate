PROBLEM DEFINITION DOCUMENT

1. BUSINESS CONTEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The project focuses on an on-demand, scheduled, and express online delivery platform 
connecting customers with multi-category vendors (restaurants, grocery stores, 
pharmacies) across different cities. 

In a highly competitive market, the cost of acquiring new customers significantly 
outpaces the cost of retaining existing ones. Consequently, long-term 
profitability, customer lifetime value (CLV), and sustainable unit economics 
rely heavily on maintaining consistent user engagement, ensuring reliable 
delivery performance, and delivering high service quality to prevent churn.

2. PROJECT OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The primary objective of this project is to perform an Exploratory Data Analysis 
(EDA) on customer registration details, order transactions, and delivery 
performance records to:
- Identify patterns, trends, and drivers of customer retention.
- Uncover friction points in delivery operations (e.g., delays, inaccuracies).
- Categorize user behavior based on device preferences, vendor categories, 
  and cohort lifetimes.
- Provide actionable, data-driven recommendations to improve customer 
  retention and unit economics.

3. KEY RUNTIME KPIs
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Based on the available data columns, we will track the following three key performance indicators:

- **Customer Retention / Purchase Frequency**: 
  Measured by tracking the time delta between successive orders per customer, 
  overall order frequency, and active lifetime (time elapsed between a customer's 
  signup_date and their latest order_date).

- **On-Time Delivery Rate (OTDR)**: 
  The percentage of orders where delivery_status is "On-Time" or delay_minutes 
  is 0. This measures operational reliability, a core driver of customer trust.

- **Order Accuracy and Experience Rating**: 
  The percentage of orders completed with "Correct" order_accuracy combined with 
  the average customer_rating (1 to 5). This directly measures customer 
  satisfaction and service execution quality.

4. KEY HYPOTHESES TO TEST
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We will test the following three behavioral hypotheses during our EDA:

- **Hypothesis 1: Delivery Friction drives Customer Churn**
  *Statement*: Customers who experience a high average delay_minutes or a 
  non-"Correct" order_accuracy in their first three orders exhibit a significantly 
  lower repeat purchase rate and a shorter active lifetime compared to customers 
  who experience on-time, accurate deliveries.

- **Hypothesis 2: High Promotion Sensitivity correlates with Higher Churn**
  *Statement*: Customers whose order history shows a high ratio of promo_used="Yes" 
  (promo-driven shoppers) have a significantly higher churn rate and lower 
  average order value (order_value_usd) once promotional discounts are absent, 
  compared to organic (non-promo) shoppers.

- **Hypothesis 3: Cross-Category and App-based Users show Higher Engagement**
  *Statement*: Customers who place orders across multiple vendor_category values 
  (e.g., buying from both Restaurants and Groceries) and use the "Mobile App" 
  as their preferred_device have a higher order frequency and longer retention 
  lifetimes than web-only or single-category shoppers.
